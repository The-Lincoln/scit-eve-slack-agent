# SCITBD Autonomous Sales Engine — 8-Step Workflow for Global Client Proposals

Source: `SCITBD_Autonomous_Sales_Engine.pdf` (15 slides). Directive: **$1.5M year-end target, 24/7 autonomous mode**.

1. **Target Audience Matrix (30+ countries)** — NGOs/Public (BD, South Asia, UAE/KSA: Logframe, e-GP, data compliance); Enterprise & Tech (US/UK/EU/AU: ERP, cloud, chatbots, audits); SaaS & Startups (NA/UK: MVP, subscriptions, automation).
2. **Strategic Personas + Dark Data** — M&E/NGO execs (spreadsheet chaos → Logframe tracking); Founders/Ops (95% dark data → 70% less manual marketing); CTOs/CIOs (legacy + ISO27001/GDPR → 99.9% uptime, secure APIs).
3. **Core Offers as Prescriptions** — Tier 1 Foundation (RBM engines, humanitarian DB, ERP/HRM/cloud with source); Tier 2 Accelerator (AI chatbot builder, content generator, analytics); Tier 3 Stability (AMC retainers, cybersecurity, marketing — 30% one-time → retainer target).
4. **24-Hour BST Marketing Plan** — Block 1 SEO/tenders; Block 2 EU ads + CTO prospecting; Block 3 US launch + sub-2h proposals; Block 4 analytics + Oceania + briefing + reset.
5. **Conversion Assets & Velocity** — 7-touch SaaS drip (Day 1 dark-data gap → Day 3 suite → Day 6 social proof → Day 9 free pilot → Day 13 objections → Day 17 urgency → Day 21 CTA); monthly 30 LinkedIn articles + 8 videos (10k views) + 3 SEO posts/day per block.
6. **Discovery-First Closing (Sabri Suby)** — diagnose 80% listen; mirror prospect language; 1-to-10 close on silent objections; cost-of-inaction math; price-down-to-daily + Suby Silence; **≥$10k lead → CEO video audit in 24h**.
7. **AgencyOS Implementation** — PHP/SQLite multi-agent backend (Hermes Skills 14 skills + AI CEO + Lead Engine); Proposal Factory (50 templates, sub-2h SLA); run_due_tasks triggers; 05:30 BST governance — daily schedule to Slack, approval restricted to CEO email, release fires cron.php → daily_activities log.
8. **Analytics & Self-Learning** — Daily Intelligence Briefing (revenue vs $1.5M, leads, ROAS, uptime); feedback-ratio auto-improvement; monthly P&L pivot (kill losers, scale winners).

Flywheel: Attract (content) → Engage (7-touch) → Diagnose & Close → Record (KPI) → Evolve (self-learn). Positioning vs traditional agency: diagnosis over pitching, 24h blocks over 9-to-5, 10–30 touches over 2–3, multi-agent over spreadsheets.
